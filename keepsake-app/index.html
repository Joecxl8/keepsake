About Keepsake

Let’s be honest: default calendar apps are boring, and missing a birthday, anniversary, or Kerala festival is not an option. Keepsake (Ormappetti) was built to take the panic out of special dates.

Created by Joe Manu Thomas, Keepsake combines dynamic countdowns, gamified redemption scores, and localized AI suggestions with zero clutter.

PWA Enabled · Malayalam Nakshatram Support · Backup & Export · WhatsApp Integration.

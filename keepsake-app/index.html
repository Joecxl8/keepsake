About Keepsake

Welcome to Keepsake (Ormappetti), a lightweight, culturally rooted application designed to ensure no milestone, festival, or important date slips past unnoticed.

Keepsake was created by Joe Manu Thomas and co-created by Joe Jose Koshy—two developers passionate about crafting clean, zero-bloat web utilities with a localized touch.

Built with plain HTML, Tailwind CSS, and vanilla JavaScript, backed securely by Vercel serverless functions and Gemini AI.
